How I use Claude as a coding and learning partner, and how I check its work: my prompts, what I verified, what I changed and mistakes I caught.

# Reviewing my SQL and Python notes with Claude

**Prompt:** I shared my SQL and Python practice notes that I had in Word document on my laptop (based on last few months of practice) and asked Claude to review them for mistakes before I published them.

**What the AI gave me:** a list of issues: typos in a join query, a SUM where COUNT was right, a vague answer about NULLs in averages, an outdated pandas function, a mixed-up explanation of real versus nominal values and private details that should not be public.

**How I checked it:** [I uploaded my Word document, and asked Claude to: compared each fix with my original exercise and re-read the corrected queries]

**What I changed:** I accepted the fixes I could confirm, added my own notes, and left out the private material. 

**What I learned:** AI is a fast second reader but I decide what is correct and what I add in shared public files.
